Artemis Program

NASA's return to the Moon. Artemis I sent an uncrewed Orion capsule around the Moon in 2022. Artemis II carries four astronauts on a lunar flyby, the step before the first crewed landing near the lunar south pole.

  • First flightNov 16, 2022
  • VehicleSLS + Orion
  • DestinationThe Moon
View on the timeline
Completed NASA

Apollo 11

On 20 July 1969, Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin became the first people to walk on the Moon while Michael Collins orbited above in Columbia.

  • LaunchJul 16, 1969
  • Crew3
  • Duration8 days
Active NASA / JPL

Voyager 1

Launched in 1977, Voyager 1 flew past Jupiter and Saturn. In 2012 it became the first spacecraft to reach interstellar space, and it still sends data home.

  • LaunchSep 5, 1977
  • Distance25B+ km
  • PowerRTG
Active NASA · ESA

Hubble Space Telescope

Released from Space Shuttle Discovery in 1990, Hubble has made more than 1.6 million observations and helped measure how fast the universe is expanding.

  • LaunchApr 24, 1990
  • OrbitLow Earth
  • Servicing5 missions
Active NASA / JPL

Mars Perseverance

Landed in Jezero Crater in 2021 to search for signs of ancient microbial life and collect rock samples. It carried Ingenuity, the first aircraft to fly on another planet.

  • LandedFeb 18, 2021
  • SiteJezero Crater
  • Mass1,025 kg

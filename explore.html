Saturn

The jewel of the solar system. Saturn is made mostly of hydrogen and helium and is so light on average that it would float in water. Cassini orbited it for 13 years before diving into its atmosphere in 2017.

  • Main rings stretch about 280,000 km across but are often only tens of meters thick.
  • Visited by Pioneer 11, Voyager 1, Voyager 2 and Cassini–Huygens.

Launch history

From Sputnik to interstellar space

Ten launches and landings that shaped the Space Age, in chronological order.

  1. Oct 4, 1957

    Sputnik 1

    The first artificial satellite, a 58 cm polished metal sphere, beeps from orbit and starts the Space Age.

    USSR · First satellite
  2. Apr 12, 1961

    Vostok 1

    Yuri Gagarin becomes the first human in space, completing one orbit of Earth in 108 minutes.

    USSR · First human
  3. Jul 20, 1969

    Apollo 11 lands on the Moon

    Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin set down in the Sea of Tranquility while an estimated 600 million people watch on TV.

    NASA · Crewed
  4. Sep 5, 1977

    Voyager 1

    Launched toward Jupiter and Saturn. In August 2012 it crossed the heliopause into interstellar space.

    NASA · Still active
  5. Apr 12, 1981

    STS-1 Columbia

    Exactly 20 years after Gagarin, the first Space Shuttle flight debuts a reusable crewed spacecraft.

    NASA · Shuttle era
  6. Apr 24, 1990

    Hubble Space Telescope

    Carried to orbit by Discovery on STS-31. A 1993 servicing mission corrected its flawed primary mirror.

    NASA · ESA
  7. Nov 20, 1998

    ISS: Zarya module

    The first module of the International Space Station launches. Crews have lived aboard continuously since November 2000.

    International
  8. Aug 6, 2012

    Curiosity lands on Mars

    A rocket-powered "sky crane" lowers the car-sized rover into Gale Crater on nylon cables.

    NASA / JPL
  9. Dec 25, 2021

    James Webb Space Telescope

    An Ariane 5 lifts off from Kourou carrying the 6.5 m gold-coated mirror to the Sun–Earth L2 point.

    NASA · ESA · CSA
  10. Nov 16, 2022

    Artemis I

    SLS sends an uncrewed Orion around the Moon and back on a 25.5-day test flight.

    NASA · Test flight
